🧧 红包发射

开发中，尚未启用。以下为拟实现规则，不代表已上线的奖励承诺。

一币一池，每轮一人

创建者选择 15 分钟、1 小时、2 小时或 4 小时的周期，发射后固定。该代币实际可领取的创作者手续费全部进入专属链上奖励金库；协议费用、交易本金及池流动性不进入红包池。普通发射不变。

谁可以参与？

快照时至少持有 1 枚完整代币的钱包，每个地址一份机会。创建者、程序地址、销毁地址和冻结账户排除。一个人可拥有多个钱包，因此这不是“一人一票”。无需另外买票，持币也不保证获奖。

如何开奖？

快照服务公布地址清单、快照区块和 Merkle 根，并将根与本轮金额写入链上。之后才请求 ORAO 随机数。合约按随机数确定唯一获奖索引，并验证钱包证明后付款。同一轮不可重新抽取或重复领取。随机数可验证；持币名单完整性仍依赖快照服务，并可由公众复核。

奖励与时间

计划最低奖励为 0.01 SOL、0.01 BABA（按原子单位换算）或 100 FAFA。未达门槛或无人符合条件时继续积累，不虚构中奖。服务延迟时开奖可能延后，不保证每到整点付款。上一轮完成后开始新的完整周期，未来收到的费用属于后续轮次。运行方另付网络费与随机数费用。

上线前

链上程序、费用收集适配器、快照运行器及端到端测试全部完成后才会开放红包发射。现有币不会自动变成红包币。正式规则和部署地址会在上线前公布。

Red envelope launches

In development—not active. These are proposed implementation rules, not a live reward promise.

Creators choose a fixed 15 minute, 1 hour, 2 hour or 4 hour cycle. All claimable creator fees for that coin go into its own onchain vault. Protocol fees, trading principal and pool liquidity are excluded.

Each eligible wallet holding at least one whole token at the snapshot gets one entry. Creator, program-owned and frozen accounts are excluded. Multiple wallets can produce multiple entries. Holdings do not guarantee a reward.

A keeper publishes the complete snapshot and commits its Merkle root and reward amount before requesting ORAO randomness. The contract verifies the selected wallet’s proof and pays once. There is no reroll or administrator withdrawal instruction. Snapshot completeness still depends on the keeper and must be independently auditable; upgrade authority is a separate deployment trust assumption.

Underfunded cycles roll forward. Minimum proposed rewards are 0.01 SOL, 0.01 BABA (in raw-unit terms), or 100 FAFA. No eligible wallets means no draw. Delays are possible; each completed round starts the next full cycle. Operators pay oracle and transaction costs separately.

Deployment, fee-collection integration, keeper operation and end-to-end validation are required before activation. Existing tokens are not automatically enrolled.
