🧧 持币红包

服务器托管版。是否开放新红包发射，以创建页的实时状态为准；展示功能不代表已完成真实发放。

一币一钱包，每轮一人

新发射的参与代币获得独立奖励钱包，SOL 或 FAFA 创作者手续费进入该钱包。协议费用、交易本金和池流动性不计入红包。现有代币不会自动加入。直接转入奖励钱包的捐赠也计入奖励。

周期与奖励

创建者选择 15 分钟、1 小时、2 小时或 4 小时。达到 0.01 SOL 或 100 FAFA 且存在合格持币者时，服务器记录一名获奖者，并支付本轮记录的全部奖励及基础代币费用。运行方另付网络费与代币账户租金，不扣运营比例。未达门槛则继续积累。服务延迟时发放可能延后；已确认付款后开始下一完整周期。

谁有机会？

最终确认快照时至少持有 1 枚完整代币，每个钱包一份机会。创建者、运行方、该币奖励钱包、程序地址和冻结账户不参与。一个人可以拥有多个钱包，因此不是一人一票。持币不保证获奖。

托管与抽奖方式

运行方持有奖励钱包密钥并控制付款。此版本没有自定义红包合约，也不使用 ORAO。服务器通过加密安全随机数选择获奖钱包，将持币名单、快照区块、金额和获奖者保存后再付款。重试使用同一获奖者；先确认旧交易状态，再决定是否重发。

快照和付款交易可公开核对，但无法独立证明运行方没有干预随机选择或名单。运行方可以控制资金，因此请勿将此机制视为无信任或可验证公平的合约。周期规则由软件记录，不由链上红包合约强制执行。奖励钱包不是用户自己的钱包；交易签名仍由用户自己的钱包批准。

Red envelope rules

Server-run custodial version. Check the creation page for current enrollment availability. A visible feature is not proof of a completed live payout.

A separate wallet for each token

Participating new SOL or FAFA launches route claimable creator fees to a dedicated reward wallet. Protocol fees, trading principal and pool liquidity are excluded. Existing tokens are not automatically enrolled. Direct donations to the reward wallet are also included.

Cycles and eligibility

Choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or 4 hours. Each eligible wallet with at least one whole token at the finalized snapshot gets one entry. Creator, operator, that token’s reward wallet, off-curve owners and frozen accounts are excluded. Multiple wallets can each qualify; holding does not guarantee a reward.

A funded cycle needs at least 0.01 SOL or 100 FAFA and an eligible holder. One winner receives the recorded quote reward and any recorded base-token fees. The operator separately pays network costs and token-account rent. No operating percentage is deducted. Underfunded cycles accumulate; delays are possible. The next full cycle begins after confirmed payout.

Operator custody and server randomness

The operator holds the reward-wallet keys and controls payments. No custom reward contract or ORAO oracle is used in this version. The server uses cryptographically secure random selection, then saves the snapshot, amounts and selected winner before sending payment. Recovery preserves that winner and reconciles the old transaction before any replacement.

Published snapshots and explorer links let you inspect eligibility and payments. They do not independently prove unbiased selection or prevent operator interference. This is not trustless or contract-enforced. Cycle settings are recorded by software. Creator fees belong to the designated custodial reward wallet; user trading transactions still require the user’s wallet signature.
